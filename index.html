03 / MAKE THE CHOICES

Build your version.

Change the pace of costs, add an investment, and decide how to pay for it. Every change updates the five-year outlook.

What changes each year?

Expense defaults use the median annual change in comparable FY22–FY25 source lines, capped at 0–15%. Historical increases include service expansions and funding changes, not just inflation. They are a starting point, not a forecast. Debt stays flat until a schedule is supplied.

Revenue assumptions

$2.1M starts from the book's FY27 new-growth assumption. Tax revenue grows at the selected rate plus these new dollars each year. This simplified model is not a certified levy-limit calculation; excluded debt, ceiling and levy capacity need reconciliation.

Department & account overrides

A department change starts in FY28 and carries forward. Account growth replaces the shared growth rate for that line.
